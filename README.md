South Region Sales Analysis (2015–2018)

Project Overview

This project analyzes sales performance in the South region using the Retail Superstore dataset from Kaggle. The objective is to identify trends, patterns, and factors contributing to sales fluctuations between 2015 and 2018.

Dataset

Dataset: Retail Superstore Dataset
Source: Kaggle
Time Period: 2015–2018
Region Analyzed: South Region

Problem Statement

An analysis of the South region revealed a significant decline in sales during 2016 compared to the previous year. While the number of orders decreased only slightly, sales revenue experienced a much larger decline, indicating changes in order value, or category performance.

Quetions

1. What are the overall sales trends from 2015–2018?
2. Which region showed the highest decline in 2016?
3. Which category contributed most to the decline in the South region?
4. What are the possible reasons behind the drop in sales?

Objectives

Analyze yearly sales trends in the South region.
Compare sales and order performance across years.
Identify categories and subcategories contributing to the sales decline.
Generate business insights and recommendations.

Key Findings

1. Significant Sales Decline in 2016
South region sales decreased substantially in 2016.
Order volume showed only a slight decrease.
The larger decline in sales compared to orders suggests a reduction in average order value.
2. Technology Category Performance
The Technology category contributed significantly to the decline.
Within Technology, the Machines subcategory experienced the largest impact.
3. Machines Subcategory
Sales in the Machines subcategory decreased by approximately 55% in 2016.
This was one of the major factors affecting overall South region sales performance.

Conclusion

The South region experienced a notable sales decline in 2016 despite only a small reduction in order volume. The analysis indicates that lower sales values per order, particularly within the Technology category and the Machines subcategory, were major contributors to the downturn. The Machines subcategory alone showed a 55% decrease in sales, making it a key area for further business investigation.This decline may be driven by a shift toward lower-value purchases, lower orders in south region impacting overall revenue growth.
